# Lentil chili (veggie chili con carne)

## Ingredientos

- [ ] 2 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1 large onion, finely chopped
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 red bell pepper, diced
- [ ] Fresh chili, finely chopped (optional)
- [ ] 2 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 1 tsp ground coriander
- [ ] 2 tbsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 1 tsp dried oregano
- [ ] ½ tsp ground cinnamon (optional)
- [ ] 2 tbsp tomato paste
- [ ] 2 x 400g cans brown lentils, drained and rinsed
- [ ] 400g can chopped tomatoes
- [ ] 250ml vegetable stock
- [ ] 400g can red kidney beans or black beans, drained and rinsed
- [ ] 1 tsp sugar or a square of dark chocolate
- [ ] Juice of ½ lime
- [ ] Salt and black pepper
- [ ] Rice, to serve
- [ ] Sour cream or yoghurt, to serve
- [ ] Fresh cilantro, to serve
- [ ] Tortilla chips, to serve

## Instructionos

1. Cook the aromatics: Heat the olive oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until soft and translucent, about 8 minutes.
2. Add garlic, pepper and chili: Stir in the garlic, bell pepper and fresh chili (if using). Cook until the pepper starts to soften, a few minutes.
3. Bloom the spices: Add the cumin, coriander, smoked paprika, oregano and cinnamon (if using) with a pinch of salt and pepper. Stir for about a minute until fragrant. Add the tomato paste and cook for another minute.
4. Add the tomatoes and stock: Pour in the chopped tomatoes and vegetable stock and add the sugar or chocolate. Scrape up anything stuck to the bottom.
5. Simmer: Stir in the lentils and beans, bring to a boil, then turn down to a gentle simmer. Cook uncovered for 15-20 minutes, stirring now and then, until the chili has thickened. Add a splash of water if it gets too thick.
6. Taste and adjust: Add the lime juice, then adjust with salt, pepper, or a bit more sugar/chocolate if it needs rounding out.

Serve: Serve hot over rice, with a dollop of sour cream or yoghurt, fresh cilantro on top, and tortilla chips on the side. Goes well with [sweet potato feta](recipe.php?name=sweet-potato-feta).

**Note:** Canned lentils are already cooked, so they only need to heat through and soak up the sauce. Don't simmer much longer than 20 minutes or they turn mushy. Like the meat version, it's even better the next day.
