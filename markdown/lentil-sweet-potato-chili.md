# Lentil & sweet potato chili (veggie chili con carne)

## Ingredientos

- [ ] 3 tbsp olive oil (1 for the sweet potato, 2 for the pot)
- [ ] 1 large onion, finely chopped
- [ ] 3 garlic cloves, minced
- [ ] 1 red bell pepper, diced
- [ ] Fresh chili, finely chopped (optional)
- [ ] 1 medium sweet potato (~400g), peeled and cut into 1-2cm cubes
- [ ] 2 tsp ground cumin
- [ ] 1 tsp ground coriander
- [ ] 2 tbsp smoked paprika
- [ ] 1 tsp dried oregano
- [ ] ½ tsp ground cinnamon (optional)
- [ ] 2 tbsp tomato paste
- [ ] 200g dried brown or green lentils, rinsed
- [ ] 400g can chopped tomatoes
- [ ] 750ml vegetable stock
- [ ] 400g can red kidney beans or black beans, drained and rinsed
- [ ] 1 tsp sugar or a square of dark chocolate
- [ ] Juice of ½ lime
- [ ] Salt and black pepper
- [ ] Rice, to serve
- [ ] Sour cream or yoghurt, to serve
- [ ] Fresh cilantro, to serve
- [ ] Tortilla chips, to serve

## Instructionos

1. Roast the sweet potato: Heat the oven to 200°C. Toss the sweet potato cubes with 1 tbsp olive oil, a pinch of salt and 1 tsp of the smoked paprika. Spread them out on a baking tray in a single layer and roast for 25-30 minutes, turning halfway, until soft and caramelised at the edges. Carry on with the chili while they roast.
2. Cook the aromatics: Heat the remaining 2 tbsp olive oil in a large heavy-bottomed pot over medium heat. Add the onion and cook until soft and translucent, about 8 minutes.
3. Add garlic, pepper and chili: Stir in the garlic, bell pepper and fresh chili (if using). Cook until the pepper starts to soften, a few minutes.
4. Bloom the spices: Add the cumin, coriander, remaining smoked paprika, oregano and cinnamon (if using) with a pinch of salt and pepper. Stir for about a minute until fragrant. Add the tomato paste and cook for another minute.
5. Add lentils and liquid: Stir in the rinsed lentils, then pour in the chopped tomatoes and vegetable stock. Add the sugar or chocolate. Scrape up anything stuck to the bottom.
6. Simmer: Bring to a boil, then turn down to a gentle simmer. Cover partially and cook for 30-35 minutes, stirring now and then, until the lentils are tender. Add a splash of water if it gets too thick before the lentils are done.
7. Add the beans and sweet potato: Stir in the beans and simmer uncovered for about 10 minutes so the chili thickens. Fold in the roasted sweet potato for the last few minutes, just to heat it through, so it keeps its shape.
8. Taste and adjust: Add the lime juice, then adjust with salt, pepper, or a bit more sugar/chocolate if it needs rounding out.

Serve: Serve hot over rice, with a dollop of sour cream or yoghurt, fresh cilantro on top, and tortilla chips on the side.

**Note:** Use brown or green lentils, since they keep their shape. Red lentils fall apart and turn it into more of a dahl. Like the meat version, it's even better the next day.
