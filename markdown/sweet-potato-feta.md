# Sweet potato feta

A side dish for [lentil chili](recipe.php?name=lentil-chili), or anything else.

## Ingredientos

- [ ] 1 medium sweet potato (~400g), peeled and cut into 1-2cm cubes
- [ ] 1 tbsp olive oil
- [ ] 1 tsp paprika powder
- [ ] A pinch of salt
- [ ] 100g feta, crumbled

## Instructionos

1. Heat the oven to 200°C.
2. Toss the sweet potato cubes with the olive oil, paprika and salt.
3. Spread them out on a baking tray in a single layer and roast for 25-30 minutes, turning halfway, until soft and caramelised at the edges.
4. Crumble the feta over the hot sweet potato and serve.
